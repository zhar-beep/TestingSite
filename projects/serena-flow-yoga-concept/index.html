Common Questions

Clear answers before you begin

Do I need to be flexible or experienced before starting?
Not at all. Sessions are paced specifically for beginners and desk workers, using props like cork blocks or a folded towel so movements meet your body where it is today.
What equipment do I actually need for home sessions?
A supportive mat is enough to start. Two firm blocks, a simple cotton strap, and a light fabric resistance band are helpful additions later—you can see why each item is useful in the Curated Equipment Hub.
Does Serena Flow sell and ship the recommended equipment directly?
No. Coaching services and physical product recommendations are kept completely separate. Equipment links send visitors to external merchants via affiliate referral links with clear disclosures, and there is no checkout cart on this website.
Is this a live studio or a portfolio demonstration?
This site is a clearly labeled Concept Project / Portfolio Exercise created inside Commerce Studio to demonstrate a clean, free-to-host Track A deliverable (1-page personal website + bio-link affiliate hub) for solo coaches and micro-creators.
