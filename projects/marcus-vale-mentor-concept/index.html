Section 06 · Common Questions

Practical answers before you start

Clear details on the E-Book editions, private mentoring preparation, speaking sprints, and international scheduling.

Should I start with the $19 E-Book or book a 1:1 mentoring call?
If you want repeatable boardroom frameworks immediately, start with Speak With Calm Authority ($19) or the Complete Clarity Bundle ($39). Book a 1:1 session when you face a specific, time-sensitive career move or high-visibility stage.
What formats are included in the Flagship E-Book and Bundle?
The $19 Flagship E-Book includes a 142-page PDF and EPUB edition plus 18 boardroom scripts. The $39 Complete Clarity Bundle adds the 90-minute studio audio companion and 12 editable memo and slide templates.
How should I prepare before a 1:1 Career Clarity session?
After reserving a slot, you complete a short five-question diagnostic brief. Writing down your central decision and 90-day goal lets us spend the full hour solving the real problem rather than recapping context.
How far ahead should organizations inquire for keynotes or workshops?
For conferences and leadership offsites, 6 to 12 weeks provides enough runway for an audience discovery call and custom material. Shorter lead times are occasionally open for virtual executive sessions.
Do you work with international leaders across time zones?
Yes. All 1:1 sessions and 4-week speaking sprints run via studio-grade remote video across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific, with morning and late-afternoon weekday slots.
