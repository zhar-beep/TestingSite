Common Questions

Clear answers before you begin

Do I need to be flexible or experienced before starting?
Not at all. Sessions are paced specifically for beginners and desk workers, using props like cork blocks or a folded towel so movements meet your body comfortably where it is today.
What props do I actually need for home sessions?
A supportive mat is plenty to begin. Two firm blocks, a simple cotton strap, and a light fabric resistance band are helpful additions over time—you can browse my exact daily picks inside My Essentials.
How do online sessions and rescheduling work?
Every live class and 1:1 session meets over a simple video link sent ahead of time. If your week shifts unexpectedly, you can reschedule any private session with at least 24 hours' notice at no fee.
Can I start with one 1:1 session before joining a series?
Yes. Booking a single 1:1 Private Mat Pilates session is the easiest way to assess your posture, talk through any stiffness, and decide whether weekly group flow or a 4-week foundation suits you best.
